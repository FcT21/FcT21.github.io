Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our standards, cadence, and policies.

How often should I schedule recurring home cleaning? +

Most homeowners prefer a bi-weekly cadence to maintain consistent hygiene and order without disruption. Weekly schedules suit high-traffic family homes with pets, while monthly services offer a comprehensive seasonal reset.

What is included in a Deep Restorative clean? +

Our Deep Restorative Clean targets neglected architectural details: hand-wiped baseboards and crown molding, interior kitchen cabinetry and pantry sanitization, bathroom tile grout scrub, doorframe scuff removal, and HEPA dust extraction.

Are your cleaning professionals insured and background-checked? +

Yes. 100% of our staff undergo rigorous local and national background checks, reference verifications, and comprehensive training. We carry full surety bonding and $2,000,000 general liability insurance for complete peace of mind.

Do I need to supply cleaning equipment or solutions? +

Not at all. We arrive fully equipped with professional HEPA vacuums, fresh color-coded microfiber towels, and plant-based, non-toxic Atelier Sage formulations that protect your surfaces, air quality, and family.

What is your satisfaction guarantee? +

We stand behind every appointment. If any area does not meet your exacting standards, notify us within 24 hours and we will return promptly to rectify it at zero charge.
