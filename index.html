Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our residential standards, home access, and policies.

Why do you use non-toxic botanical formulations instead of harsh chemicals? +

We believe hygiene should protect life, not burden it with respiratory irritants. Our eco-certified botanical formulations eliminate harsh chlorine and toxic solvents while delivering hospital-grade sanitation, preserving both indoor air quality and delicate architectural finishes like natural stone, hardwood, and quartz.

What is the purpose of the Deep Restorative Clean before recurring home care? +

Conceived as an essential "first-clean baseline reset", our Deep Restorative Clean meticulously addresses accumulated grime in neglected architectural details—hand-wiping baseboards, interior cabinetry turnover, range-hood degreasing, and grout restoration. It brings your space to the Kosmios standard so subsequent recurring visits effortlessly sustain harmony.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning, and how do you handle home access and pets? +

Many of our residential clients prefer having their homes cared for while they are at work or running errands. We work comfortably with secure lockboxes, smart door keypad codes, or trusted key-holding arrangements. We are entirely pet-friendly and ensure your pets remain comfortable, secure, and undisturbed throughout our visit.

Do you provide all cleaning supplies, equipment, and vacuums? +

Yes, absolutely. Our specialists arrive fully equipped with commercial-grade HEPA filtration vacuums, color-coded microfiber systems to prevent cross-contamination, and our signature non-toxic, plant-based cleaning formulations. You do not need to provide anything.

What insurance, bonding, and satisfaction guarantees protect your clients? +

100% of our cleaning specialists undergo rigorous background vetting and are bonded. We carry $2,000,000 in commercial general liability insurance. Furthermore, every visit is backed by our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee: if any detail does not meet your exacting standards, we return promptly to rectify it at zero charge.
