Residential Cleaning Services

From regular maintenance visits to deep restorative resets and moving days, every visit is guided by honest care, attention to detail, and safe plant-based products.

Our Core Service Plan I

Recurring Maintenance

The easiest way to keep your home fresh, healthy, and stress-free. We establish a reliable routine that takes everyday cleaning off your hands so you can focus on what matters most.

Frequency Options: Weekly · Bi-Weekly (Most Popular) · Monthly
  • Kitchen countertops, sinks, backsplashes, and exterior appliances wiped clean
  • Complete bathroom disinfection, shower and tub scrub, and mirror polishing
  • Multi-stage HEPA vacuuming and damp botanical mopping on all hard floors
  • High-touch points sanitized: door handles, light switches, and cabinet pulls
  • Thorough dusting of baseboards, window sills, shelves, and light fixtures
  • Consistent cleaning specialist so you always know who is in your home

Recommended First Clean Plan II

Deep Restorative Clean

A comprehensive top-to-bottom reset for your home. Ideal as a first visit before starting a recurring plan, or whenever your space needs deep attention to built-up dirt and dust.

Recommended As: Initial Reset Before Recurring Plans or Seasonal Care
  • Detailed hand-wiping of baseboards, door frames, mouldings, and trim
  • Deep degreasing inside the oven and range hood with non-toxic solutions
  • Interior refrigerator and pantry shelving cleaning and sanitizing
  • Intensive scrub of bathroom tile and grout to remove stubborn buildup
  • Deep HEPA dusting of ceiling fans, blinds, vents, and neglected corners
  • Leaves your home at a fresh baseline that is easy to maintain over time

Realtor & Relocation Plan III

Move-In / Move-Out Clean

Connected with Ottawa realtors, property managers, and moving families. An exhaustive turnover clean for empty homes, ensuring a smooth security deposit return, great listing photos, or a spotless fresh start.

Service Scope: Complete Empty-Home Turnover & Inspection-Ready Checklist
  • Deep interior and exterior wiping of all kitchen and bathroom cabinets and drawers
  • Inside appliances cleaned: refrigerator, freezer, oven, dishwasher, and washer/dryer
  • Interior window glass, window sills, tracks, and sliding door tracks
  • Wall scuff spot-cleaning, detailed baseboards, and entryway washdown
  • Full bathroom sanitization for an immediate, worry-free moving day
  • Detailed checklist that satisfies realtors, landlords, and new owners
