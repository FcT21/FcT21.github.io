Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

At Kosmios, order is a form of care. That philosophy extends to how we protect and honor your personal data in accordance with Canadian federal privacy regulations (PIPEDA).

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 · Jurisdiction: Ottawa & National Capital Region, Ontario, Canada

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Kosmios Architectural Care (“Kosmios”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) provides high-standard residential and commercial cleaning services across Ottawa and the surrounding National Capital Region (NCR). We are firmly committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and security of the personal information you entrust to us.

This Privacy Policy sets out our data collection practices, our administrative safeguards, and your statutory rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation in Ontario.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information solely when voluntarily provided by you through our online service inquiry forms, email correspondence, or direct service agreements:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, email address, optional contact telephone, and company/organization name (for commercial partners).
  • Property & Space Specifications: Approximate square footage, room counts (bedrooms/bathrooms), frequency preferences, and special architectural or material care requirements.
  • Communication Records: Notes, preferences, or inquiries submitted via our contact forms.

We do not collect financial payment card numbers directly on this website, nor do we harvest biometric or sensitive demographic identifiers.

3. Purpose of Collection & Use

Kosmios collects and utilizes your information strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:

  • To formulate and deliver accurate service quotations and scope estimates.
  • To coordinate logistical scheduling and technical visits for private residences and corporate facilities.
  • To maintain ongoing communication regarding your scheduled cleaning maintenance.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and insurance verification duties applicable in Ontario.

4. Data Processors & Technical Safeguards

We employ modern organizational and technical safeguards to shield your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss:

  • Encrypted Form Transmission: Inquiries submitted via our website forms are transmitted securely via Formspree using industry-standard TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption.
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers: Our website contains no behavioral tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or cross-site data brokers.
  • Confidentiality Standards: All Kosmios team members and specialists are background-checked, insured, and bound by strict confidentiality and non-disclosure standards.

5. Absolute Non-Disclosure & Prohibition of Sale

We will never sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties, marketers, or data brokers. Your data is accessed strictly on a need-to-know basis by authorized Kosmios coordinators to perform your requested service.

6. Retention & Data Minimization

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, or as mandated by statutory accounting and commercial liability requirements. When personal data is no longer necessary, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you possess clear rights regarding your personal information held by Kosmios:

  • Access: You have the right to request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request the rectification of any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw your consent for future communication at any time.
  • Erasure: You may request the permanent removal of your contact details from our records, subject to legal record retention duties.
